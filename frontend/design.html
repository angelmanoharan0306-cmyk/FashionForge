Measurement Guide

All measurements are taken on the finished garment in inches with garment laid flat. Chest is measured 1" below armholes across bust apex. Waist is measured across natural narrowest waistline. Hip is measured 8" below natural waistline. Shoulder is measured from outer seam to seam across back. Arm Hole is circumference along armscye curve.
