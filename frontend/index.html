Studio Capabilities

One workspace for the entire creative cycle.

Every feature of FashionForge is engineered to keep experimentation clear, visual, and immediately grounded in real garment construction.

01 / SILHOUETTES

Component-Based Modeling

Compose bespoke dresses and outfits by assembling bodices, skirts, set-in sleeves, and finished necklines calibrated to anatomical landmarks.

02 / LIGHTING

2.5D Volumetric Draping

Experience authentic fabric depth through directional key lighting, bust fullness highlights, sleeve contouring, and undulating skirt drape flutes.

03 / PRECISION

Dual Technical Flat CAD

Instantly generate clean vector technical flats for front and back construction with princess seams, armscye attachments, and center-back zipper details.

04 / INTELLIGENCE

Live Cost & Specifications

View real-time itemized price estimates and rule-based style recommendations that adapt automatically as you select premium fabrics and silhouettes.
